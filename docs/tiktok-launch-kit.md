# MOGG BATTLE — TikTok launch kit

## Positioning
One sentence: **photo duels with friends in Telegram**. A player starts a duel, shares it, and people vote. No crypto, no AI claims, no promise of money. Keep the first video about the social challenge, not the app's feature list.

## Content angle
Use the voice of a real player sharing something they tried. You can say you found it, tried it, or are inviting people to test it only if that is true. Do not claim you were paid for an ad unless there was a real paid deal. If this is your own project, don't invent a sponsor or fake independent endorsement. You can keep the founder story out of the video and make the content about the challenge itself.

## First 5 short videos
1. **Challenge:** on-screen text: “Кто из нас заберёт батл?” Show two consenting participants' photos, the actual duel flow, then ask viewers to challenge a friend.
2. **Friend group:** “Отправил это в чат — теперь они спорят, кто выиграл.” Use a real chat/duel only with permission; blur private messages.
3. **Pick a side:** show the two photos and ask “1 или 2?” Then show the real voting flow. Don't fake votes or results.
4. **Reaction:** show a real result and reaction, if participants agree to be shown.
5. **Referral:** “Нашёл игру, где можно вызвать друга на фото-батл. Заходи по ссылке — сыграем.” Add your personal referral link in profile/bio. Explain the +100 Points only if the current referral offer is still active and clarify they are in-game points, not cash.

## 12–18 second edit template
- 0–2s: hook, no logo intro.
- 2–6s: two real photos / a friend challenge.
- 6–11s: screen recording of the actual game and voting.
- 11–15s: outcome or invitation.
- End card: “Вызови друга в Telegram — ссылка в профиле.”

Use a simple native edit: phone screen recording, captions, ordinary room lighting, real reactions. Avoid AI portraits, fake testimonials, fake chat screenshots, and elaborate promotional graphics.

## Caption options
- “Кто выиграет — я или друг? Проверим 👀 Ссылка в профиле, кидай вызов своему человеку.”
- “Новый способ поспорить с друзьями: два фото, один батл. Кто победит?”
- “Нашёл прикольную штуку в Telegram — можно устроить фото-батл с другом. Кто готов?”
- Referral: “Залетай по моей ссылке, создавай профиль и сыграем. Если рефералка активна, обоим начислят игровые Points после создания профиля с фото.”

## Bio
“Фото-батлы с друзьями ⚡
MOGG BATTLE в Telegram ↓”

## Measure after 5–10 videos
Track views, profile visits, link clicks, opened game, completed profile, first duel, and referral completions. Views alone do not prove the idea works. Keep the hook that produces real players, not just views.

## Before posting
- Test the Telegram link from another account/device.
- Ensure a new user understands how to start a duel.
- Don't post anyone's face without consent.
- Verify the referral link is the personal link from your in-game profile.
- Confirm the game doesn't show NPC-vs-NPC or fake activity.
